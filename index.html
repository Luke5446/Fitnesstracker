Home strength plan

Four days a week, thirty minutes each. Two 4 kg dumbbells and the treadmill.

Good to know

Your first two weeks

Do 2 rounds instead of 3 and don't worry about the slow tempo yet — just learn the movements. Mild soreness the next day is normal; sharp pain isn't. From week 3, move up to 3 rounds.

Which days to train

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday or Saturday works well. Keep a rest day after Day 2. A 20‑minute treadmill walk on rest days is a bonus, not a requirement.

How to keep progressing

Muscle only changes when the work gets slightly harder over time. Change one thing every week or two, in this order:

  • Slow the lowering phase to 3–4 seconds
  • Add reps, up to 15 per exercise
  • Add a 4th round if it still fits in 30 minutes
  • When legs and glutes feel easy, a second pair at 8 kg is the best upgrade
What to expect

Strength and posture changes show up within about 3 weeks. Visible definition usually takes 8–10 weeks of consistent sessions. Protein helps muscle show sooner — a palm‑sized portion with each meal is a simple target.
